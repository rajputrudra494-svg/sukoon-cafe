०१ the name

सुकून su·koon

noun — Hindi / Urdu

  1. The quiet that settles in when nothing is rushing you.
  2. What you feel on the second sip, not the first.

We built Sukoon out of things that calm us down: dark stone walls laid piece by piece, a rust-red arch you walk under to get in, cane chairs, terrazzo tables, and far more plants than anyone can sensibly water.

Come for a V60 at noon or stay for dal makhani at eleven. The kitchen is fully vegetarian, close to half of it can be made Jain, and the coffee bar has a lab of its own.
