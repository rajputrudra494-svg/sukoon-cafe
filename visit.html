०३ good to know

Before you come over.

Is everything vegetarian?

Yes. The whole menu is vegetarian, kitchen and bar.

Can you do Jain food?

43 dishes on the kitchen menu can be made Jain. They're marked with a J on the menu page, and there's a filter for them.

Do I need to book?

Not on most days, but weekend evenings fill up. A quick WhatsApp through the form above or a call to +91 93552 55294 saves you the wait.

Where do I park?

There's a free parking lot, and valet is available.

Is it good with kids, or with a wheelchair?

Both. There are kid-friendly options on the menu, and the entrance and seating are wheelchair accessible.

How do the Studio and the Turf work?

Slots and rates change through the week, so call or WhatsApp us and we'll tell you what's open.

Are you open late?

Usually 12 PM to 11:30 PM, every day. During festivals like Navratri we stay open much later; watch @sukoon.surat for those nights.
